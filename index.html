About

More than getting a date — we run the whole booking.

Given Agenda is the point of connection between talent and opportunity. We connect artists with promoters, venues, festivals, brands, and private events, and manage the full process around every hire.

That means commercial negotiation, contracts, invoicing, payment coordination, flights, lodging, transportation, riders, hospitality, and production logistics — across Puerto Rico, the United States, and international markets. We work with flat fee, buyout, and all-in contracting models, adapting to each event and market.
