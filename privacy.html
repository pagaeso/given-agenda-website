Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Given Agenda ("Given Agenda," "we," "us," or "our") provides talent booking, artist representation, and entertainment logistics services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit givenagenda.com or contact us directly, including via email or WhatsApp.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any details you share when you reach out about a booking, event, or partnership inquiry — whether through email, WhatsApp, or any contact form on this site.

We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or advertising pixels on this site beyond what may be required for basic hosting and security by our infrastructure providers.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to booking inquiries and communicate about potential or confirmed events.
  • To coordinate the logistics of a booking, including contracts, payments, travel, and production details.
  • To communicate with artists, promoters, venues, and partners involved in a booking.

WhatsApp Communication

If you communicate with us via WhatsApp, that conversation is used solely to manage the inquiry or booking you initiated. We do not use WhatsApp messaging to send unsolicited marketing, and we do not sell or share your WhatsApp contact information with third parties outside the scope of coordinating your booking.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share relevant details with third parties directly involved in executing a booking (such as venues, promoters, or production vendors) only as needed to fulfill that booking, and with service providers who help us operate (such as email or hosting providers), under obligations to protect your information.

Data Retention

We retain contact and booking information for as long as reasonably necessary to manage the business relationship and comply with legal, accounting, or contractual obligations.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing us at givenagenda@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to givenagenda@gmail.com.
